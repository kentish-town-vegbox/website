One last step

Thank you for filling in our sign-up form

You should receive an email with all your registration details shortly.

The final step is to set up a Direct Debit mandate with our payment provider, GoCardless, so we can start taking your weekly payments.

GoCardless mandate set-up

We will pack your first veg box for you after you have set up a Direct Debit with GoCardless.

Set up your Direct Debit

Once you have set up the payment, we'll send you a welcome email with more details about when and where to collect your first bag of veg!

Please contact us at info@vegbox.org.uk or 07815 771 939 if you have any problems.
