Seasonal Veg Calendar 2027

£15 for members, £18 for non-members

Take a month-by-month journey through the seasons with our first-ever Vegbox calendar! Tune into the natural rhythms of the seasons, discover new ways to enjoy what’s growing, and brighten up your kitchen.

Beautifully designed by Ellie Paganini, the calendar features photos from the packing yard, original illustrations and our mischievous mascot, Brassica.

Inside you’ll find

  • What's in season: a month-by-month guide to seasonal fruit and vegetables
  • Recipe inspiration: ideas to help you make the most of the produce available
  • Illustrations and cartoons: original artwork by Ellie Paganini
  • Day planner: space to keep track of the plans, events and holidays

Whether it’s hanging in your kitchen, given as a gift, or used as a gentle nudge to eat more seasonally, we hope it brings a little curiosity and enjoyment to your year.
