Your sustainable food journey starts here

Sign up to Kentish Town Vegbox

Fill in the form below and we'll email you a confirmation straight away. One of the team will then be in touch within a few days to confirm your collection point, set up your weekly Direct Debit, and let you know when your first bag will be ready.

We will use the number provided to notify you when your veg bag is ready to be collected.

The fruit option adds a weekly portion of fruit to your veg bag.

Payment details

Once you have completed this form you will be asked to set up a Direct Debit mandate with GoCardless, a secure online payment system.

Direct Debit is the most common and hassle-free system for our members. The weekly payment goes out every Monday, before the Wednesday when you collect your bag.

We will pack a veg bag for you once you have set up a Direct Debit with GoCardless. You can expect your first bag a week after the payment is set up – we will confirm by email when your membership has started.

If you have any problems setting this up, please get in touch at info@vegbox.org.uk.

Holiday policy

If you don't need a bag, let us know by 5pm on Tuesday of the week before and we will pause your payments for the weeks you take off.

We need to know a week in advance because that's when we order the produce from the farms. We order exactly the right amount so we don't waste any produce.

Please still let us know if you are away even if you miss the deadline, and we will make sure to redistribute your veg.

We'll only use your details to run your Vegbox membership – we never share them with anyone else. Prefer to sign up by email? Write to us at info@vegbox.org.uk.
