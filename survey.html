Vegbox member survey – winter 2026

Dear Vegbox member,

As a member-led co-operative, Vegbox runs occasional surveys to collate members' views and help shape our service. We really value your views and we act on what we learn. All our surveys are completely confidential and anonymous, and we delete all the survey data once the analysis has been completed.

If you're uncomfortable about answering any of our questions, don't worry – every question is optional. Having said that, the more responses we get, the more accurate the results will be and the better we can improve.

This year we have added a few new questions to help our buyer get the right balance of vegetables in your bag through the seasons, so your answers will go straight into what we order from our growers.

Many Vegbox members are, in effect, households containing more than one person, so where this is the case we ask that at least one other adult from the household also completes the survey.

The survey will only take about 5 minutes. And please do remember that you can give us feedback (both positive and negative!) about Vegbox at any time, by emailing info@vegbox.org.uk or by calling 07815 771 939.
