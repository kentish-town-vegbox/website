Look after each other

The Solidarity Fund

Fresh fruit and veg shouldn't be a luxury. We believe everyone should have access to food that's good for people and the planet – and the Solidarity Fund is our community's way of making that real.

What is it?

In June 2022, the members of Kentish Town Vegbox voted to create the Solidarity Fund to subsidise veg bags for members of our community facing hardship. If money is tight, the fund pays for a low-income discount on your weekly bag – no questions asked, just tick the box when you sign up. We also accept Healthy Start vouchers.

Inequality in Camden and Islington is a serious issue, with both boroughs ranked among the most unequal communities in the country – and with the cost of energy, food and rent rising, some of our members are feeling the crunch. We don't want anyone to have to give up their membership because of financial constraints.

"Vegbox has been so kind – not many organisations offer a low-income discount, and they even delivered to me for free while I was bedridden after breaking my leg and hand."

— a Vegbox member

"When we were on the low-income discount, me and my family were still able to eat healthy organic vegetables. We don't receive the discount any more, but we stayed with Vegbox because of the generosity and kindness they showed us."

— a Vegbox member

Chip in

Donations from members and supporters subsidise discounted bags directly – and for every contribution you make, Vegbox adds another £1 of its own. A small regular amount goes a long way:

£1 a week

Helps subsidise a Small veg bag for a neighbour who needs it

 £3 a week

Helps subsidise a Standard veg bag for a neighbour who needs it

 £5 a week

Helps subsidise a Family veg bag for a neighbour who needs it

Donations are handled securely by Liberapay, a non-profit donation platform – you can also give a one-off or different amount there.

Need the discount yourself? Just tick the low-income rate box on the sign-up form – no questions asked. Existing members can email info@vegbox.org.uk.
