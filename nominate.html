Kentish Town Vegbox Ltd · AGM, 30 November 2026

Director nomination form

If you would like to join the Board of Directors of Kentish Town Vegbox Ltd, please fill out this form.

If you would like any more information about joining the Board, please phone our Board Co-Chair Maggie on 07984 972 257.

Membership of the Board of Directors involves the commitment to attend meetings – we expect members to attend 80% of our monthly meetings per year – and to play an active role in fulfilling the aims of the co-operative.

Please fill out this form by end of day Friday 27 November.

Please write a few words giving your reasons for wanting to stand, and any useful experience or skills that you would bring as a Director of the Board.

Nominations have closed

Nominations for the Board at the 2026 AGM have now closed. If you have any questions, please call Maggie on 07984 972 257.

Thank you for standing

Your nomination has been sent to the Board. We will be in touch before the AGM on Monday 30 November.
